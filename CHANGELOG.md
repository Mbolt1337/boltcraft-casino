## Early preview — November 2026

The first public look at BoltCraft Casino. Not released yet: the jar comes with the first release, once it has been
checked in survival from scratch, on a dedicated server and with friends.

- Vault, cashier, chips worth 1, 10, 100 and 1,000, the manager's key and protected casino blocks.
- Slot machine with three reels, a lever, win and jackpot animations; return to player 93.39%.
- Roulette with a single zero for any number of players, run by a villager croupier who talks in speech bubbles.
- 64 decor blocks, furniture you can sit on, food, drinks and a serving tray.
- A neon lounge and a grand hall in world generation, to be found and claimed.
- A guide book, 13 achievements, English and Russian.
