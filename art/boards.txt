# Large wall boards, drawn by hand: the CASINO marquee signs, the giant chips and the BoltCraft banner.
# art/boards.py turns them into multi-block wall decor, like the neon boards of art/neon_signs.txt.
#
# "= tile <name>"            a 16x16 piece of the marquee board: frame corners, edges and the red field. The
#                            frame is lit from the top left; chasing bulbs are added along its middle.
# "= font <size> <letter>"   a bulb letter: '#' is a gold stroke cell four pixels square with a bulb in it.
# "= drawing <name>"         a whole board drawn pixel by pixel in palette colours; 'Y' marks glowing tube.
# "= sign <name> <width> <height> <font> <text>"  a marquee board of width x height blocks.
#
# Light comes from the top left everywhere.
