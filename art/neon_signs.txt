# Neon signs, drawn by hand. art/neon.py turns every tube pixel into a small glowing cube standing off the wall
# and paints a halo in the tube's darker shade round it.
#
# Tube colours: M pink, T cyan, Y yellow, V green, P red, U purple, W white. In tile drawings the other
# characters are palette colours (art/palette.txt) drawn as they are.
#
# "= icon <name>"           a 16x16 icon sign, tubes only
# "= tile <name>"           a 16x16 piece of a panel's board and gold frame
# "= letter <name>"         a 5x7 (or narrower) tube letter for the panels, '#' is the tube
# "= panel <name> <blocks>" a wide board: one line of tokens "<letter>:<colour>" or a number of blank pixels,
#                           centred on the board, letters standing on row 4
