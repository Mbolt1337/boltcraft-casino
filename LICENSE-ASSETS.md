## What counts as an asset

- Textures, models, block states, sounds, fonts and language files under `src/main/resources/assets/` and
  `src/generated/resources/assets/`.
- Building templates (`*.nbt`) under `src/main/resources/data/boltcraft_casino/structure/`.
- The art sources: pixel grids, palettes, building plans and Blockbench models under `art/` and `models/`.
- The images and animations of the project page under `docs/media/`.

The Python scripts under `art/` that turn the grids into images are code and fall under the MIT licence in
`LICENSE`. The grids, palettes and plans they read are assets.

## What you may do

- Play the mod, in single player and on servers.
- Record, stream and publish videos and screenshots of it.
- Include the unmodified mod jar in modpacks, provided you credit BoltCraft Casino and link to this repository
  or its official download page.
- Fork the repository and build it to study, fix or extend the code, and send changes back as pull requests.

## What you may not do without written permission

- Sell the mod or its assets, or put them behind a paywall on their own.
- Re-upload the mod to other download sites as if it were yours, or under another name.
- Use the textures, models, sounds or building templates in other projects, mods, resource packs or games.
- Present modified assets as the official BoltCraft Casino.

Ask through the repository's issues if you want to do something this does not cover.
